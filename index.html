Tokyo

Alone among millions. A chance encounter with a city.

Tokyo - Story

On a Tokyo train, a woman says she’s almost home. She meets the narrator’s eyes for a moment, then looks away. The scene holds the feeling that gave rise to the song: being surrounded by people and still feeling far from them.

The woman can also be heard as Tokyo itself. After the train come rain, neon and a bar. By the time the narrator asks “Mind if I stay?”, the city begins to feel like someone they’ve just met.
